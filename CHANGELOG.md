# Changelog

## [1.2.1] - 2026-11-23

### Changed
- Renamed to **Owned Mats** (addon folder `OwnedMats`), since CurseForge does not allow "Auctionator" in another project's name. Settings saved under the old name are not carried over.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-21

First public release.

### Added
- **Not Learned** tab in the Best Crafts window: unlearned recipes of the open profession with Recipe Cost, Profit/Craft and Payback (crafts needed to earn back the recipe price).
- **Scan AH** in the Not Learned tab also searches for the recipe items.

### Fixed
- Recipes with an unknown cost or payback stay at the bottom of the list in both sort directions.

## [1.1.1]

### Fixed
- The **Best Crafts** button stays visible when a UI skin addon restyles the Professions window.

## [1.1.0]

### Added
- Price of missing mats and Cash Profit lines in the Professions window.
- Best Crafts window (`/aom list`) with filters, sortable columns, tooltips and a live **Scan AH** button.
